# Handoff: INFERNO has no transition that defers a work item out of a running intent

**What is wrong.** The single writer `state-transition.cjs` offers claim, unclaim, block and unblock for an intent, and complete and review for an item, but nothing that defers or skips one item. `close-intent` refuses while any item is not `completed` or `done` (`state-transition.cjs:171-179`, `state-ledger.cjs:8`, `isComplete`), so a parked status such as `on_hold` still blocks the close. The orchestrator text says "Skipped items STAY in the ledger as `skipped`" (orchestrating-builders section 5), yet `skipped` is in neither `COMPLETE_VALUES` nor `PARKED_VALUES`, so the ledger would read it as open work and the intent could never close.

**Evidence.** Skoft_CRA, 2026-11-10: the campaign lead's exit bar (only highs build before the launch) asked two running intents to defer items to the first update. Both had to hand-edit `.specs-inferno/state.yaml`, removing the items from `work_items` and moving their specs to `deferred/` (`the-article-14-filings-are-due-on-time-and-complete` commit a22cebd6b, seven items; `the-declaration-of-conformity-is-complete-for-every-stated-law`, six items), against the rule "NEVER hand-edit state.yaml".

**How to fix.** Add `state-transition.cjs defer-item --intent <id> --item <id> --reason <why>`: it refuses an item that is in flight or completed, refuses while an open item still depends on it unless `--rewire <item>=<new-dep>` names each dependent's new edge, sets `status: deferred` with `deferred_at` and `deferred_reason`, and adds `deferred` to a set that `close-intent` and `check` treat as closed for this intent. `archive-intent` keeps deferred items in the archived intent with their reason, so the next plan reads them. `frontier` and `select` print deferred items as such and never offer them. Tests: defer then close succeeds; defer of a completed or dispatched item refuses; a dependent without `--rewire` refuses.
