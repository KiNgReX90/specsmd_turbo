# Handoff: the planner turns a shared file, a regenerated seal or a wanted order into a depends_on_intents edge, and nothing asks an edge what the later intent reads that the earlier one creates, so 17 of one host's 34 edges held intents back and moved its end estimate by five days

Row 0bk. Measured on the first host on 2026-11-24 in its open plan on `main`, written by five planner runs on the planner text of the build installed there. The tip `0f29e1b3` holds the same `src/` as that build, `a8873ebb` (`git diff --stat a8873ebb 0f29e1b3 -- src` is empty).

## What is wrong

`S/` below is `src/flows/inferno/agents/orchestrator/skills/orchestrate/scripts/`.

1. **"Depend" has no test, and the edge rule starts from a shared file.** "**depend**: a distinct deliverable that must run after another." (`src/flows/inferno/agents/planner/agent.md:93`). "A file shared with a pending intent is an intent-level `depends_on_intents` edge only when the later intent calls, tests, extends or rewrites what the earlier adds; it may point at an `on_hold` intent, never at a completed one or in a cycle." (`:166`, both the same in `src/flows/inferno/commands/inferno-planner.md`). Any later edit of a shared file extends or rewrites what the earlier adds, and "must run after" takes any order a planner prefers. The classify step meets other intents only through file overlap: "the file-level sweep is only for a plausible overlap" (`agent.md:89`). The Codex text is the same in other words: "**independent** when scopes and ownership proceed separately" (`src/flows/inferno/codex/skills/specsmd-inferno-planner/references/procedure.md:59`), so shared ownership alone rules independence out.
2. **An edge's reason is prose that nothing reads.** "every dependency names what makes it real" (`agent.md:15`) is the only demand for evidence. It lands in the entry comment's cross-intent decisions (`:184`), the handoff's `Depends on: <intent-id>, because <one line>` (`:201`) and a brief paragraph. The brief's front matter `depends_on` (`src/flows/inferno/agents/planner/templates/brief.md.hbs:5`) and the state's `depends_on_intents` hold ids only.
3. **A manifest cannot hold a real cross-intent read.** `pathErrors` (`S/run-manifest-paths.cjs:66-86`) refuses a `context.required` or `reads` path the base lacks unless an upstream item of the same intent owns it. So an item cannot cite the file another intent creates, and the one true reason for an edge has no place a script could check.
4. **Nothing checks an edge.** `S/run-plan-lint.cjs` reads the items' `depends_on` (`:242`, `:251-253`, `reach` at `:331`) and never `depends_on_intents` or a brief's `depends_on`. Its `overlaps` (`:342`) compares the items of one intent. `S/run-scan-plan.cjs` and the scanner's plan cross-check (`src/flows/inferno/agents/scanner/agent.md:36-52`) check no edge.
5. **Each edge holds the later intent through the earlier one's whole run.** `claim` refuses with `DEPENDS_UNMET` until every prerequisite is completed (`S/state-transition.cjs:264-284`), and `select` prints `blocked <id>  waiting on <ids>` (`S/run-intents.cjs:172`). The plan's cross-check waits with exit 3 while a prerequisite is unmerged (`S/run-scan.cjs:243-260`). A false edge so costs the earlier intent's build, reviews, gate and ship, and a chain of them runs a plan in series.

## How to fix

One test decides an edge: an item of the later intent reads a file, symbol, field or migration that an item of the earlier intent creates and the base lacks. A shared file, a shared list or pin, a regenerated seal or manifest and a look one restyle extends are folds the second to land resolves. A sweep waits for the surfaces whose rules it lifts, since it reads what they create, and no surface waits for a sweep it reads nothing from, so a sweep goes last and never mid-chain.

1. **The rule text.** `agent.md:93` and its copy: "must run after another" becomes "reads what another creates". `agent.md:166` and its copy become: "**Ownership is recorded truthfully.** Items that recompile one tree form a linear chain, labelled compile serialization in the technical notes. A `depends_on_intents` edge needs an item whose `builds_on:` names a file, symbol, field or migration that an item of the prerequisite creates and the base lacks; shared files, lists, pins and regenerated manifests are folds the second to land resolves, never edges, and a sweep goes last. Prefer disjoint ownership." The `on_hold`, completed and cycle clause moves into the lint. Codex `procedure.md:59` takes the same test for **independent** and **depend**. Its `:60`, `:122` and the words "and intent" in `:164` go, since the lint then holds them.
2. **The budget.** Counted with the test's own split (`src/__tests__/unit/inferno/text-budget.test.ts:16-17`, `:35`, `:43-49`) on an install of the tip: the Claude planner holds 3123 of 3125 words, the Codex planner (its toml, `SKILL.md` and `procedure.md`) 2904 of 2906. Both Claude changes are word-neutral: `:93` keeps 4 words, `:166` keeps 70. The Codex change frees 19 words by its deletions and spends 20 on its new clause, 2905 of 2906.
3. **The evidence.** The later intent's item manifest gets `builds_on:`, entries of intent, item, path and an optional symbol, in the style of `reads` (`src/flows/inferno/agents/planner/templates/work-item.md.hbs`, after the `reads` block at `:110`). `readItemSpec` already parses the manifest, and the item is what reads. `pathErrors` accepts a path the base lacks when the item's own `builds_on` names it and an item of that intent owns it (pass `builds_on` through at `run-plan-lint.cjs:393`).
4. **The lint.** `edgeFindings` in `S/run-plan-lint.cjs`, called from `lintPlan` before its old-form return (`:365`), since the edges live in `state.yaml` whatever the brief's form. For each id in the linted intent's `depends_on_intents` that is live and not completed:
   - one of its items names that intent in `builds_on`;
   - each entry names an item of that intent whose `ownership.editable` holds the path (`editableReader`, exported from `S/run-manifest-paths.cjs:119`);
   - the base lacks the path, or lacks the named symbol in it (a `git grep` of the base, or `fs` as `pathErrors` reads);
   - the item's own `context.required` or `reads` cites the path.
   It also refuses an id neither the ledger nor the archive holds, and a cycle, the rules that leave the text. It reads the ledger and the archive itself (`lib.readLedger`, `isComplete`, `archivePaths` and `archivedIds` from `S/state-archive.cjs`), not through `S/run-intents.cjs`, which would close a require cycle through `S/run-frontier.cjs`. Findings in the file's form, for example `FINDING brief: depends on <A>, but no item names it in builds_on; a shared file, list, pin or manifest is a fold, never an edge` and `FINDING <item>: builds_on <path>, which the base already holds; name the symbol <A>/<item> adds, or drop the edge`. An edge to a completed or archived intent is history and passes, so the check is inert at `frontier` (`S/run-frontier.cjs:154-155`), where the claim already made every prerequisite complete.
5. **The tests**, in `S/run-plan-lint.test.cjs`. `ledger()` (`:146`, its `depends_on_intents: []` at `:160`) takes a prerequisite intent `card-ledger` with an item `ledger-store`. `plan()` (`:174`) writes that item's spec with `item()` (`:55`), `withItem` (`:197`) splices `builds_on:` in, and `assertOneFinding` (`:200`) holds each refusal to one line. The cases:
   - an edge whose `builds_on` names a symbol the base lacks in a file the base holds passes, and so does a new path that `context.required` cites;
   - an edge whose only evidence is a path both intents own and the base holds is refused, `/the base already holds/`;
   - a regenerated manifest the base holds (`definitions/pack-manifest.json` written in the fixture) is refused, with no symbol and with a symbol the file already holds;
   - an edge no item names in `builds_on` is refused, `/no item names it in builds_on/`;
   - a path no item of the prerequisite owns is refused, and so is a cycle;
   - a completed prerequisite gives no finding, at plan-lint and at frontier.
   The text pins go beside those of rows 0bi and 0bj in `src/flows/inferno/agents/planner/scripts/team-work-item-contract.test.cjs`: every planner text holds `builds_on:` and "never edges", for `agent.md` and `codexSkillFile("specsmd-inferno-planner", "references/procedure.md")`.
6. **The measurement.** The planner text change is measured on both hosts with `evals/planner-ab` before it is ported, as the standing rule under section 2 of the index asks.

### An edge holds every item of the later intent for one item's read

- With the edge restored as the host's oracle asks (`every-machine-starts-on-the-cut-and-earns-its-motion: src/lib/walkthrough/step-slide.test.ts`, in the row's own form), one item of 14 reads what the motion intent creates.
- `claim` still refuses the whole intent with `DEPENDS_UNMET` until the motion intent is completed (`S/state-transition.cjs:277-283`), and `select` prints it `blocked` (`S/run-intents.cjs:172`).
- An item's `depends_on` names only items of its own intent: the frontier readies an item when each of them is in that intent's completed ids (`S/run-frontier.cjs:169`). A host has no way to hold one item on another intent.
- The motion intent has 15 items and walks its cases on machines that still need weston, per the same oracle. Its run, gate and ship are the hold.
- The 14 items form one linear compile chain, and the reading item is its root (`depends_on: []`, every other item reaches it through `depends_on`). So an item-level hold pays here only when the planner places the reading item last in its chain.
- The first intent also waits on three other open intents (`state.yaml` lines 2186-2188), so on this plan the edge costs time only when the motion intent ships after all three.

Fix: with `builds_on` in place, the hold moves to the item. `claim` refuses `DEPENDS_UNMET` only when every open item names an unmet intent in `builds_on` or reaches such an item through `depends_on`. The frontier prints such an item `waiting <item> on intent <id>` until that intent is completed and the tree holds each `builds_on` path, which a fold of the base brings. The planner places an item with `builds_on` on an unmet intent last in its compile chain. The case `claim-intent refuses while a prerequisite intent is still open` (`S/state-transition-claim.test.cjs:57`) keeps its refusal for an intent all of whose items are held and gains a case that claims an intent with one held item. `S/run-frontier.test.cjs` gains a case where that item waits on the intent while its siblings are ready, and is ready once the prerequisite is completed and its path stands in the tree.

**Until it is built.** An owned path the base lacks that an item of another open intent creates keeps its edge; the first host restores `every-machine-starts-on-the-cut-and-earns-its-motion` for `src/lib/walkthrough/step-slide.test.ts`, which its audit `2cc90cb24` had cut. The planner places the item that reads such a path last in its compile chain.
