When an Acme Shop order that was paid fully or partly with a gift card is refunded, the gift card
share goes back onto the card it came from instead of the refund being refused. A refund of some
lines of an order gives each payment back its share of those lines, as decision record 0003
describes. The customer gets one refund email that names the amount back on the gift card, the
card's new balance and the amount back on the payment card. The refund shows up in the nightly
accounting export as a line type of its own. A card that has expired since the order gets the
amount back anyway, and its expiry moves to 30 days after the refund when it would end earlier.
